The course's working definition, stated precisely because everything else
depends on it: a lost or abandoned game is one that was **commercially
released** and can **no longer be normally obtained or played through its
original distribution channel or intended platform**. A game that's merely
old, obscure, or still commercially available doesn't qualify just because
it's unfashionable — plenty of decades-old games are one click away on a
storefront right now, and none of those are this course's business.

The course centres on games from the **1990s–2010s**, across **PC, console,
and browser/Flash** — not because nothing was lost before or after that
window, but because that's where recoverable evidence, surviving
communities, and workable emulation overlap. Online and server-dependent
games come up as a preservation problem worth understanding, but they are
not something you'll be asked to recover: without a surviving server, there
is usually no artifact left to excavate.
