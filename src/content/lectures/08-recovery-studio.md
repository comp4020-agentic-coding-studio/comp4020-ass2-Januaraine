From here, the semester shifts from worked examples to your own artifact.
You choose one qualifying lost or abandoned game — PC, console, or
browser/Flash, per week 1's definition — and open a recovery attempt using
whichever methods from weeks 2–7 fit it: emulation, archival research,
file-format reading, save-file analysis.

There's a short check-in this week rather than a full brief, because the
point is to pick something real and hit its first obstacle while a tutor is
in the room. A well-documented failed recovery is a legitimate outcome by
the end of the semester — it is not a legitimate outcome to arrive at week
11 having not tried.

## Outline

- choosing an artifact that actually qualifies under week 1's definition
- scoping a recovery attempt to what's achievable in the remaining weeks
- picking your first method, and your first obstacle
- what "stuck" looks like, and when to change approach rather than push through
