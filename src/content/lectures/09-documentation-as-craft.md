An archaeological report is judged on whether a reader who never saw the
artifact can tell what was found, how, and how confident to be about it. This
week is about writing that report as you go rather than reconstructing it
from memory afterwards: what to log the moment something works (or doesn't),
what evidence to keep even if it looks irrelevant at the time, and how to
write about a dead end without it reading as a confession of failure.

The same structure applies whether your artifact ends up fully playable,
partially recovered, or provably unrecoverable — the difference is in the
findings, not the shape of the report.

## Outline

- logging as you go, not reconstructing afterwards
- evidence worth keeping: screenshots, hex dumps, dead ends and all
- writing a failed step so it reads as a finding, not an apology
- what your studio notes need to contain by week 11
