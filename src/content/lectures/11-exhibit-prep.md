The recovery exhibit is a public-facing presentation, not an internal status
report — the audience hasn't read your notes and doesn't owe you the benefit
of the doubt about what "mostly working" means. This week is about the
translation: cutting nine weeks of hex dumps and false starts down to the
handful of things a non-expert audience actually needs in order to
understand what you found and why it matters.

Presenting a fully working recovery and presenting a well-documented failure
take the same care. Either way, the exhibit should let someone who's never
touched the artifact leave knowing what it was, what happened to it, and
what you did about that.

## Outline

- who the exhibit is actually for, and what they don't already know
- choosing the handful of moments that carry the whole story
- presenting a partial or failed recovery without hedging it into mush
- rehearsing the two-minute version before the twelve-minute one
