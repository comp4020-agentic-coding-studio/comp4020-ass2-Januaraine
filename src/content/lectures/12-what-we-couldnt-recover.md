Not every artifact gives up its secrets in a semester, and this course has
never asked that of you — only that a failure be documented well enough that
someone else could pick it up. The closing lecture looks back across past
attempts (including gaps still open in *Meridian Drift*'s own record) to
draw out what a well-documented failure actually looks like versus one that
just gives up early.

This is also the semester's last word on the ethics thread from week 6: what
digital archaeology can responsibly claim to have "recovered," and what it
should describe more carefully as "reconstructed," "partially restored," or
"still lost."

## Outline

- what's still missing from `Meridian Drift`, five years of community effort later
- the difference between a well-documented failure and an abandoned one
- recovered, reconstructed, or still lost: being precise about the claim
