## Before the studio

Install the platform toolchain from the setup guide linked on this page once
it's published: a DOS/PC emulator, a console emulator, and a hex editor.
Bring one game you personally suspect is "lost" — you'll test that guess
against the definition today.

## In the studio

Working in pairs, you'll sort a shared list of a dozen games into lost,
merely old, and genuinely ambiguous, using week 1's definition rather than
gut feel. The ambiguous pile is the point — that's where the definition's
edges actually get tested.

## Afterwards

Keep your sorted list. The reasoning you used on the ambiguous cases today
is exactly what week 8 asks you to apply to your own chosen artifact.
