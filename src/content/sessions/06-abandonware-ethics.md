## Before the studio

Have a draft of your Field Report, even a rough one, open and in front of
you. This studio is working time, not a lecture.

## In the studio

Bring your draft's weakest section — usually the provenance and legal
framing — and get it checked against week 6's lecture before you submit.
A tutor reading it today is considerably cheaper than a marker flagging it
after the deadline.

## Afterwards

Field Report due today, in whatever format the brief specifies.
