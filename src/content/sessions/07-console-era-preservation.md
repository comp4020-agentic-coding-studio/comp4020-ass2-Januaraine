## Before the studio

No hardware needed — every dump used today comes from a supplied archive,
exactly as your own console-platform recovery work will if you choose one.

## In the studio

You'll be given three archived dumps of the same cartridge, from three
different upload sources, with slightly different checksums and provenance
notes. The task is to work out which one you'd actually build a recovery
on, and defend that choice.

## Afterwards

If you're considering a console-platform artifact for your own project,
this is the exact judgement call you'll need to make with real archives.
