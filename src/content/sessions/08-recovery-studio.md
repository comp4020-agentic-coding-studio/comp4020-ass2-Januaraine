## Before the studio

Come with two or three candidate artifacts, not just one — the studio time
today is partly about ruling weaker candidates out.

## In the studio

For each candidate, you'll check it against week 1's definition, confirm at
least one surviving source actually exists for it, and get a tutor's read on
whether it's scoped realistically for the weeks remaining. You leave with
one committed choice.

## Afterwards

This choice is what weeks 9–12 and the Recovery Exhibit are built on.
Changing it later costs real time, so raise doubts today rather than in
week 10.
