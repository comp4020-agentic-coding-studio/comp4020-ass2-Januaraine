## Before the studio

Bring whatever you already know about where your artifact's files came
from — even "I don't actually know" is a valid starting point today.

## In the studio

Working from week 10's method, you'll search out forum threads, wiki
history, and upload notes connected to your own artifact, and assess what
they actually support versus what they merely assert.

## Afterwards

Whatever you can't verify by the end of today should be written into your
recovery notes as an open question, not quietly dropped — the exhibit and
report are stronger for naming it.
