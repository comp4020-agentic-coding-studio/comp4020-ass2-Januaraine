## Before the studio

Have a rough version of your exhibit ready to present, even unpolished —
the point of a dry run is to find the problems before week 12, not to hide
them.

## In the studio

Short run-throughs, timed, followed by direct feedback: what was clear to
someone seeing this for the first time, and what needed the presenter to
fill in a gap live.

## Afterwards

Fix what today's audience got stuck on. The Recovery Exhibit is due next
week.
