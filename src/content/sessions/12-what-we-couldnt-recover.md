## Before the studio

Submit your written report ahead of the session, per the Recovery Exhibit
brief, and arrive with your presentation ready to run without setup time
eating into your slot.

## In the studio

Exhibits are presented to the full group and any guests, in the order set
on the day. Whether your artifact ended up recovered, partially recovered,
or documented as unrecoverable, the standard is the same: can the room tell
what you found and why you trust it.

## Afterwards

This is the last scheduled session of the semester. Marking follows the
Recovery Exhibit brief's holistic criteria.
